David is the requester and the *only* intended user of Autonom-OS. Everything he
asks for should be optimised for one person's speed and comfort, never for
generality, multi-user support, or reuse.

- He writes his asks in **English**, but he is a **Spanish speaker in Colombia**
  (currency in pesos, `14.000` dot-thousands convention, Spanish loanwords
  surfacing in his English). **Do not infer product language from ask language** —
  an ask written in English does not mean he wants an English UI or English voice
  input.
- He states preferences twice when they matter to him. In the original ask he
  repeated both "I don't want anything complicated" and "I don't want to pay for
  anything". Treat repeated phrasing as a hard constraint, not a nice-to-have.
- He self-hosts: server and database on his own PC, phone as the client. He is
  comfortable with that shape, so proposals do not need to avoid self-hosting.
